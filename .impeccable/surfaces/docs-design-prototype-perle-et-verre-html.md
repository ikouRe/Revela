Surface: first proof of the Revela app. Mode: Operate. Screens: Accueil, Scanner, Résultat (two notes out of 20), Routine, Discussion, Profil. Sample data only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One liquid object carries the truth across the whole app. The category shows a static score badge on a list of tiles; Revela refuses that. The droplet is the score: it falls from the scan, lands, shows the product note, and a second droplet, the person's skin, meets it to give the compatibility note.

OWN-WORLD: Rose-tinted pearl ground with peach and rose light, deep plum ink, one raspberry-coral accent. Soft rounded display face for scores and titles, plain UI face for reading. Translucent shells, near-opaque lists and verdicts. Iridescent chrome appears only at a score reveal. Five-tab bar with a curved notch that travels to the active tab, Accueil in the centre.

STORY: The visitor sees where to scan, scans, reads why the product earns its note, then chooses to see whether it suits their skin, and reads the reasons and sources.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Accueil. Greeting top left, large droplet top right, one sentence, full-width primary Scanner action, then routine and recent scans, tab bar at the bottom.

FORM: Native-shaped phone app prototype, position 1 of 1, direction pinned by the owner. seed: seed:9d34eebc4ee74e74

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
