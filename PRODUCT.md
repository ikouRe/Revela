# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

One Revela design on Android and iOS, built with Flutter, checked on both systems from the start.

## Users

People who want the truth about the products the beauty industry sells them. They pay close attention to what is in a product and whether it suits their own skin. The product is written in French first.

## Product Purpose

Revela reveals the truth about a beauty product. You scan it and see how good it is, then whether it suits your skin, with the responsible ingredients and their sources. Success means knowing within seconds whether a product is for you, and why.

## Positioning

Other apps give a score. Revela shows why: every result carries its reasons, its ingredients and its sources, with no black box. Tagline: "Sans te mentir."

## Operating Context

Used on a phone, in a shop or at home, with a product in hand. Scanning has to work with one hand and quickly. Phase 1 data comes from Open Beauty Facts and CosIng; the compatibility rules are Revela's own sourced table.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Entry: sign in, or scan straight away without an account.
- Main navigation: Accueil, Routine, Scanner (centre), Discussion, Profil. My bathroom products live inside Routine.
- Result: a product score out of 20 first, then a compatibility score out of 20 with a personal verdict (Compatible, Prudence, Déconseillé, Inconnu). The compatibility score can never contradict the verdict.
- Analysis without a barcode through a photo of the ingredient list, with the text editable before analysis.
- With an account: profile, routine, favourites, history, settings, Discussion.
- Discussion is a small social network where people share opinions, experiences and before/after selfies. Photos stay private unless the person chooses to post. Publishing needs an account; reporting, blocking and moderation are required.
- Later phases: authenticity, carbon footprint, assistant. Their scores and pages stay honest about what is not available yet.
- Undecided: how the product score is computed beyond ingredients, and which source could supply product reviews.
- Web comes later, only if the mobile app works.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Revela. Tagline: "Sans te mentir."
- Art direction chosen by the owner: Perle et verre.
- Bottom navigation with five tabs and Scanner in the centre.
- Animated design, with 3D where it is possible.
- Logo chosen by the owner: the name "revela" with a drop flowing from the arm of the r (`../brand/`). The r alone is the icon and never sits next to the name.

## Evidence on Hand

- Business rules RG-01 to RG-62 in the Notion Pilotage page.
- First draft of 22 screens in `../brand/screens/`, useful as an inventory only: the owner rejected its style.
- No real users, reviews, testimonials, press or statistics exist. Never invent them, and mark sample data as such.

## Product Principles

1. The truth comes first: never reassure without evidence. Show "analyse insuffisante" rather than a guess.
2. Every score explains itself with reasons and sources.
3. Personal beats generic: the answer is about this person's skin.
4. Information, not medical advice: the mention stays on every verdict screen.
5. Personal data and photos stay private by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Verdicts always combine colour, icon and text, and stay understandable in greyscale. Skin tones and skin concerns are represented without judgement. Motion respects the system's reduced-motion setting.
